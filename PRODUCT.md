# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

People who encounter Ashley Willis through developer advocacy, open source,
social media, or professional communities and want a quick, trustworthy way to
find her official profiles.

## Product Purpose

Provide one memorable home for Ashley's online presence. Visitors should be
able to recognize her voice, choose the right platform without friction, and
leave with a clear sense of her personality.

## Brand Personality

Playful, candid, and confident. The voice is warm and self-aware, with humor
that feels personal rather than performative.

## Anti-references

Avoid loud neon creator pages: fluorescent palettes, constant motion, glow
effects, and visual noise that compete with the links. The site should not feel
like a generic social-link template wearing an attention-grabbing skin.

## Design Principles

- Lead with Ashley's point of view rather than the visual language of any one
  social platform.
- Make every destination immediately scannable and effortless to choose.
- Let humor add character without obscuring meaning or slowing the task.
- Prefer a few intentional, memorable decisions over a crowded collection of
  effects.
- Treat accessibility as part of the brand's welcome, not a final compliance
  pass.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Maintain strong text and control contrast, visible keyboard
focus, clear link names, comfortable touch targets, semantic structure, and a
reduced-motion experience for every nonessential animation.
